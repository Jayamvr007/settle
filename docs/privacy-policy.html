Privacy Policy for SettleKaro

Last updated: March 26, 2026

Overview

SettleKaro ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal expense splitting application. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Google Sign-In: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google. This is used solely for authentication.

App Data

  • Groups: Group names and member information you create
  • Expenses: Expense details including title, amount, category, and splits
  • Settlements: Payment records between group members

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Authenticate your identity
  • Store and sync your expense data across devices
  • Calculate balances and settlements

Data Storage

  • Your data is stored securely in Firebase Firestore
  • Data is associated with your Firebase Authentication ID
  • Each user can only access their own data

Data Sharing

We do NOT:

  • Sell your data to third parties
  • Share your data with advertisers
  • Use your data for marketing

We may share data only if:

  • Required by law
  • Necessary to protect our rights

Third-Party Services

We use these services:

  • Firebase Authentication — For secure sign-in
  • Firebase Firestore — For cloud data storage
  • Google Sign-In — For authentication

These services have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy Policy

Data Retention

  • Your data is retained as long as you use the app
  • You can delete your account and data at any time through the app settings

Security

We implement security measures including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS)
  • Firebase security rules limiting data access
  • No storage of payment credentials

Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here with an updated date.
